- **2026-07-29, batch 3 of 10 (117 groups, counts 4-5), `references/word_reviews/token_triage/batch_3_r2_triage.md`**: verdict tally KEEP 105, WHITELIST 10, REPLACE 2.
- **"steel" (II Sam 22:35, Job 20:24, Ps 18:34) — REPLACE flag, bottles/wineskins pattern.** Geneva 1599 reads "bowe of brasse" at 2 Sam 22:35 (not steel); underlying Hebrew נְחוּשָׁה (nechushah) = bronze/copper alloy. Axis 1 period (attested EModE), Axis 2 source-anachronism (carbon steel postdates the Iron-Age referent). Proposed alternate: "brass" — also the period witnesses' own word.
- **"silk" (Prov 31:22, Ezek 16:10/13) — REPLACE flag, bottles/wineskins pattern.** Geneva 1599 does NOT read "silk" at Prov 31:22 — it reads "fine linen and purple" for the same Hebrew. Real trade silk from the Far East had not reached the Levant in the era Proverbs/Ezekiel describe. Axis 1 period-attested, Axis 2 source-suspect/anachronism. Proposed alternate: "fine linen".
- **`word_whitelist.md` coverage gap (affects whitelist-slot suggestions generally):** the file only lists words a review pass actually flagged (4464 reviewed words) plus proper nouns (3602) — NOT the full English vocabulary of the restored text. Common everyday words that never triggered a rarity/review flag (e.g. "evening", "consent", "happened", "fins", "vagabond"-as-synonym-candidates) are simply absent, even though they're obviously fine words. A "none fits" whitelist-slot answer is legitimate and expected for many ordinary synonym choices — it does not mean the synonym is bad, just unreviewed. Don't mistake absence from word_whitelist.md for a negative judgment on the word itself.
- Ten groups this batch got WHITELIST verdicts as unique/hard-to-substitute referent terms: pilots, plumbline, ranges, sackbut, sardius, shaft, shipmen, undersetters, bulwarks, fleshhooks.
