# Round 7 Rare-Word Review — King James Agent Suggestions (Entries 51–100)

Dual-era authenticity notes precede each word block only where the axis
verdicts are non-trivial; otherwise see the compact advice line. Axis 1 =
translation-era (1611 English); Axis 2 = source-era (biblical-world referent).
Per binding project rule, KJV.db itself is never cited as proof of period
status — Geneva 1599 spelling variants, continuous-use reasoning, and known
EModE vocabulary (Tyndale/Bishops'/Vulgate-derived Latinisms) are used instead.

## overflown
- KJ proposal: I Chronicles 12:15 — These are they that went over Jordan in the first month, when it had overflown all his banks; and they put to flight all them of the valleys, both toward the east, and toward the west.
- KJ proposal: Job 22:16 — Which were cut down out of time, whose foundation was overflown with a flood:
- KJ proposal: Daniel 11:22 — And with the arms of a flood shall they be overflown from before him, and shall be broken; yea, also the prince of the covenant.
- alternates: overflowed, drowned, overwhelmed
- advice: keep
- Axis 1: period — "overflown" is simply the strong past-participle of "overflow" (cf. Geneva's own "ouerflowed" at Job 22:16, same verb family), a standard EModE inflection.
- Axis 2: source-era — river/flood overtopping its banks (Jordan, a wadi-flood) is an ordinary ancient Near Eastern hydrological event; no anachronism.

## palmerworm
