- **1490 rush, rushed, rushes, rusheth, rushing** — "rush" the plant (a reed/bulrush, cf. Isa 9:14, 19:15 "rush") is homonym-merged in this inflection group with the verb "to rush" (rushed, rusheth, rushing), which dominates the count — excluded under the verb rule.
- **1527 straw, strawed** — "straw" the noun is plant material (dried grain stalks, Gen 24:25), but the group also carries "strawed," the verb "to strew/scatter" (Ex 32:20; Matt 21:8) — excluded under the verb rule.
- **1700 begotten** — genealogy-adjacent (lineage/descent term, Gen 5:4 "after he had begotten Seth"), but it is the past participle of the verb "beget" — excluded under the verb rule.
- **1896 hind, hinder, hindered, hindereth, hinds** — "hind/hinds" names an animal (female deer, Gen 49:21; Job 39:1), but the inflection group conflates it with the unrelated verb "to hinder" (hinder, hindered, hindereth) — excluded under the verb rule; the two are homonyms stemmed together, not the same word.
