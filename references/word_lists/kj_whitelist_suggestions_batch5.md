## Borderline / not recommended

- **2082 fly** (animal/verb) — "flies" (swarms of flies, Exodus 8) names the insect, but "fly"/"flying" is repeatedly used as a finite verb ("didst fly upon the spoil," "he did fly," "the sparks fly upward"). Excluded per the verb rule even though the insect sense is a genuine animal referent.
- **2130 spring** (location/verb) — "spring of the waters" and "spring of the day" use it as a noun naming a water-source/place, but the dominant use across the group ("Spring up, O well," "springeth," "springing," "spring forth") is the finite verb "to spring up/grow." Excluded per the verb rule.
- **2321 fish** (animal/verb) — "fish," "fishes," "fish's" name the creature (clear animal referent, agrees across witnesses), but "fishing" is a verbal/gerund form tied to the activity of fishing ("I go a fishing," John 21:3). Excluded per the verb rule because the inflection group mixes the creature-noun with the verb-derived form.
