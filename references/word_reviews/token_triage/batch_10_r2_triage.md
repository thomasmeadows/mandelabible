## enough — 34 uses
- verdict: KEEP
- whitelist: **abundance** (476x) — Deuteronomy 28:47 "for the abundance of all things" carries the same "more than sufficient" sense. It cannot replace *enough* one-for-one (abundance implies surplus/riches, not "as much as needed") but is the nearest whitelisted noun for the idea.

- reason: KEEP. *Enough* is common Anglo-Saxon-root EModE vocabulary, attested identically in Geneva and Tyndale at this verse; no synonym in any slot improves on it — abundance overstates, sufficient sounds legalistic where the original is plain speech ("I have enough, my brother").
