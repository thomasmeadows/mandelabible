# Token triage — batch 4 of 10 — ROUND 2 (revised suggestion protocol)

Rules on all 117 inflection groups from `batch_4_r2_input.md` (counts 5–6), under
the owner's 2026-07-29 directive that every group — whatever the verdict — must
carry all three Capability 3b suggestion slots (whitelist / witness / own).
Witness readings were pulled fresh from local copies of Geneva1599, Tyndale,
Wycliffe, Darby, ASV, YLT, DRC and Webster for one representative occurrence per
group (Tyndale is frequently "(missing)" — its OT is incomplete, a known corpus
quirk). Whitelist corpus-frequency figures are word-boundary counts over
`KJV.db`'s 66 canonical books. **Verdict tally: 108 KEEP, 6 WHITELIST, 3 REPLACE.**
Two REPLACE findings are significant: **"tongs" at Exodus 25:38** (every period
and modern witness reads "snuffers" — a wick-trimmer — for this candlestick
implement, not fire-tongs) and **"transformed" at Matthew 17:2 / Mark 9:2**
(every witness without exception reads "transfigured" at the Transfiguration;
"transformed" only belongs at Romans 12:2, which is correctly the group's third
citation). "drams" at I Chronicles 29:7 is flagged as a probable unit-of-measure
mismatch (apothecary weight vs. the Persian daric coin the Hebrew and Ezra's own
usage elsewhere in this corpus indicate). All three are advisory findings for
owner review, not final rulings — none can veto a memory per the Premise

## folk — 5 uses
- verdict: KEEP
- whitelist: **people** — Genesis 12:2 "and I will make of thee a great people" (freq. ~15,001). "People" is the safer, more frequent noun, but Genesis 33:15's own sense ("some of the folk that are with me," Esau's retinue) is a diminutive, familiar collective that "people" flattens.
- witness: Geneva1599 and Tyndale both read "folke" at Genesis 33:15 ("leave with thee some of my folke") — the same word, same spelling family, unanimous with the base text.
- own: **folk** stands — it is the KJV's own word (also Prov 30:26, Jer 51:58, Acts 5:16 "sick folks") and names nothing anachronistic; a feeble folk of badgers, a nation's folk, and a crowd of sick folk are all sound Axis-2 referents.
- reason: Genuine period collective noun, still in living (if literary) use; "people" is the only real whitelisted alternative and it loses the familiar, kin-like register Esau's line needs. KEEP.
