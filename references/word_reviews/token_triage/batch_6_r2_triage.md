## perfectly — 7 uses
- verdict: KEEP
- whitelist: **throughly** — whitelisted (e.g. 2 Timothy 3:17 "throughly furnished unto all good works"); corpus-common intensifier that could stand in the same adverbial slot as "perfectly" in Jer 23:20's "ye shall consider it perfectly," though it changes the sense from completeness-of-accuracy to completeness-of-thoroughness.

- own: "perfectly" itself — attested in Tyndale NT and Geneva OT/NT alike as ordinary EModE; no better period word improves on it.
- reason: KEEP. "Perfectly" is common, unremarkable Early Modern English (it appears in Acts 18:26 and Matt 14:36 in the same restored text) naming nothing anachronistic — full understanding, full healing, more exact instruction are all sound biblical-world senses. "Throughly" is close in register but shifts the sense from accuracy to thoroughness, and Geneva's "plainely" is unattested on the whitelist, so neither improves on the standing word.
